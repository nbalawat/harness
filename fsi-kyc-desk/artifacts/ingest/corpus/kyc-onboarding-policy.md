# Corporate Client Onboarding — KYC Review Policy (v3.2)

(Source: kyc-onboarding-policy.md)

## Process
1. A case is opened when a client submission arrives with its document package.
2. The document package MUST pass a completeness check against the Document
   Checklist before any review begins. Incomplete cases are returned, not parked.
3. A risk score is computed from the Risk Rating Matrix. Scoring is mechanical:
   no analyst judgment enters the score itself.
4. A risk assessment memo is drafted for every case, summarizing beneficial
   ownership, jurisdiction exposure, sanctions screening results, and the
   computed score, citing the specific policy provisions applied.
5. Decision authority:
   - Low risk (score < 40): a KYC analyst may approve.
   - Medium risk (40-69): a senior analyst must approve.
   - High risk (>= 70): escalates to a Compliance Officer; requires their
     explicit approval AND a documented rationale.
6. No case may change status without an explicit human approval recorded with
   the approver's identity and timestamp. Automated approval is prohibited.
7. Rejected cases must record the rejection reason; the client relationship
   team is notified.

## Roles
- KYC Analyst: opens cases, runs checks, approves low-risk cases.
- Senior Analyst: approves medium-risk cases.
- Compliance Officer: approves high-risk cases and all escalations; owns policy exceptions.
- Auditor: read-only access to everything, including the full decision trail.

## Record keeping
Every state change, approval, rejection, and memo version is retained
indefinitely and must be reviewable by internal audit and regulators.
