1. Summary

Meridian Freight Holdings B.V. is a Netherlands-incorporated freight forwarding group whose file computes to a weighted risk score of 76.00 and falls in the High band. An adjudicated sanctions true-positive against a second-tier holder independently forces the High band and fixes approval authority at Compliance Officer level [1]. The document package is complete against the applicable checklist; six of six applicable items are on file [2].

2. Ownership and structure

Ownership is evidenced to the second tier only. The declaration on file covers holders at or above the twenty-five per cent threshold as the checklist requires [2]; beyond the Curaçao intermediate, no further attribution is evidenced. That residual opacity is not a documentary gap but a scoring input, and is reflected in the entity-structure raw score of 72 [3]. The structure chart supplied on 28 July satisfies the conditional requirement triggered by ownership depth of two.

3. Sanctions exposure

Screening returned one match against a second-tier holder, adjudicated a true positive at 04:12 BST on 28 July. Policy requires that a true positive force the High band irrespective of the weighted total and that the file may not be decided below Compliance Officer tier [1]. The adjudication record and screening vendor reference are retained in the case file.

4. Expected activity

The questionnaire declares €180m of annual throughput across eleven corridors, three of which appear on the elevated corridor schedule. This is consistent with the stated business model and does not, on the evidence before the desk, indicate layering [4].

5. Matters for the approver

The approver should weigh whether the residual second-tier opacity is tolerable given the sanctions finding, and must record a written rationale as a condition of any High-band approval [5]. If approved, the next review falls due six months from the decision date.
