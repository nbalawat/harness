# Document Completeness Checklist (v2.0)

(Source: document-checklist.md)

Required for EVERY corporate case:
- Certificate of incorporation
- Register of directors
- Beneficial ownership declaration covering all owners of 25% or more
- Proof of registered address (within 3 months)

Additionally required when:
- Ownership chain depth > 1: structure chart down to natural persons
- Regulated industry: copy of the operating license
- Expected cross-border activity: expected activity questionnaire

A case failing completeness is RETURNED with the list of missing documents.
Completeness is a mechanical check; analysts may not waive items. Exceptions
require a Compliance Officer policy exception, which is itself recorded.
