TABLE OVERVIEW
=================================================================

1. CASES
   Primary case entity tracking status, risk assessment, and lifecycle.
   Addresses: Case opening (REQ-001/002), completeness tracking (REQ-004),
             policy versions (REQ-070), next review (REQ-093).

2. RISK_SCORES
   Immutable record of all factor inputs, raw scores, and final score.
   Addresses: Mechanical risk scoring (REQ-016-028), factor breakdown,
             reproducibility (REQ-069), factor versioning (REQ-071).

3. DOCUMENTS
   Document inventory with required/conditional status and receipt tracking.
   Addresses: Document checklist (REQ-003-011), completeness enforcement (REQ-004).

4. MISSING_DOCUMENTS
   Immutable record of documents missing when a case fails completeness.
   Addresses: Failed completeness return with itemized list (REQ-012, REQ-089).

5. ASSESSMENT_MEMOS
   Versioned AI-drafted memos with approval status and machine-drafted label.
   Addresses: AI memo drafting (REQ-029-039), citations (REQ-031-032),
             machine-drafted label (REQ-033-034), approval adoption (REQ-035),
             version retention (REQ-039, REQ-067).

6. APPROVALS
   Decision records with required role tier, decision maker, and rationale.
   Addresses: Role-based approval authority (REQ-041-043), explicit human
             approval (REQ-044-045), auto-approval prohibition (REQ-046),
             role capture (REQ-047), rejection reason (REQ-048).

7. COMPLIANCE_EXCEPTIONS
   Policy exception records granted by Compliance Officer.
   Addresses: Mechanical check enforcement (REQ-014), policy exceptions (REQ-015).

8. AUDIT_TRAIL
   Append-only immutable event log for all case actions.
   Addresses: Complete action trail (REQ-065), immutability (REQ-066),
             indefinite retention (REQ-067), full reviewability (REQ-068).

9. ESCALATIONS
   Escalation chain tracking for high-risk cases.
   Addresses: Escalation chain (REQ-060), escalation recording (REQ-061),
             SLA evaluation (REQ-095).

10. NOTIFICATIONS
    Addressable notification records (at-risk, escalation, rejection).
    Addresses: Rejection notification (REQ-049), at-risk notification (REQ-051),
              at-risk flagging (REQ-057), audit trail of notification (REQ-063).

11. SLA_TRACKING
    SLA windows, at-risk thresholds, and breach records.
    Addresses: SLA requirements by risk band (REQ-052-054), case-ready
              timestamp (REQ-055), at-risk flagging (REQ-057), breach
              recording (REQ-058), SLA pressure prohibition (REQ-062).

12. USERS
    Role-based user registry with authentication identity.
    Addresses: Role-based access (REQ-075), role-specific permissions
              (REQ-078-081), authenticated identity (REQ-083), local
              user store (REQ-084).

=================================================================
KEY DATA MODEL DECISIONS
=================================================================

1. VERSIONING
   - Assessment memos are versioned (REQ-039) via version column + immutable rows.
   - Policy versions are stored on cases (REQ-070) to freeze the applicable rules.
   - Risk scores include the matrix version for reproducibility (REQ-069).

2. IMMUTABILITY
   - Audit trail rows are never updated or deleted (REQ-066).
   - Missing documents recorded once at failure (REQ-012) are immutable.
   - Assessment memo versions are immutable once created (REQ-039).
   - Approvals are recorded once and immutable (REQ-044-045).

3. ROLE-BASED APPROVAL
   - Approvals table records the role held at decision time (REQ-047).
   - Required role tier is recorded separately from actual role (REQ-085).
   - Compliance Officer decisions require stored rationale (REQ-043).

4. SLA TRACKING
   - Case-ready timestamp is separate from created_at (REQ-055).
   - SLA breach is permanent and not cleared by later decision (REQ-058).
   - At-risk flagging is recorded at 80% threshold (REQ-057).
   - SLA windows are tied to risk band at the time of computation (REQ-052-054).

5. AUDIT TRAIL DESIGN
   - Single audit_trail table captures all action types (policy change, status change, approval, etc.).
   - Before/after values support full change history (REQ-065).
   - Timestamp + performer identity enable complete traceability (REQ-045).

6. MEMO ADOPTION
   - Approval of a memo records approval_by_user_id on the memo itself.
   - The approver "adopts" the text (REQ-035); no separate memo_approval table.
   - Prior versions remain immutable in the table (REQ-039).
