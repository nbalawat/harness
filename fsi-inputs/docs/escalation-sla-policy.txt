ESCALATION AND SLA POLICY - KYC OPERATIONS (v1.4)

Service levels by risk band, measured from case-ready (completeness passed):

  Low risk:     decision within 48 business hours.
  Medium risk:  decision within 24 business hours.
  High risk:    Compliance Officer decision within 8 business hours.

At 80% of an SLA window consumed without a decision, the case is flagged AT
RISK and the assigned approver is notified. A breached SLA is recorded on the
case permanently and appears in the weekly operations report.

Escalation chain for unactioned high-risk cases: assigned Compliance Officer
-> Head of Financial Crime -> COO. Each escalation step is recorded with who
was notified and when.

Nothing in this policy permits skipping an approval to meet an SLA.
