Customer says their scheduled Atlas export fails every night with token_expired, but manual exports work fine. What should I tell them?

The AssistantAutomated — not a person09:41

Scheduled exports authenticate with a service token, which is a separate credential from the interactive session token used for manual exports. Service tokens expire ninety days after issue and do not renew themselves.1

Ask the customer to rotate the token under Settings → Integrations → Atlas → Rotate service token, then to open the nightly schedule and save it once more. The re-save is what binds the new credential; rotation on its own leaves the schedule holding the expired one.23

Should the job still fail, the schedule was in all likelihood created by an account since deactivated, and its ownership must be reassigned.
