Customer says their scheduled Atlas export fails every night with token_expired, but manual exports work fine. What do I tell them?

✨
Copilot Automated — not a human 09:41

Scheduled exports authenticate with a service token — a different credential from the interactive session token used for manual exports. Service tokens expire 90 days after issue and don't auto-renew. 1

Ask the customer to rotate it under Settings → Integrations → Atlas → Rotate service token, then re-open the nightly schedule and save it once. The re-save binds the new token — rotating alone leaves the old one on the schedule. 2 3

If it still fails, the schedule owner is likely a deactivated account and ownership needs reassigning.

Hi Priya,

Thanks for the detail — the difference between your manual and scheduled exports is the credential each one uses. Manual exports run on your interactive session; scheduled exports run on a service token, which expires 90 days after issue and doesn't renew. That expiry is what produces token_expired overnight.

To fix it tonight: open Settings → Integrations → Atlas, choose Rotate service token, then re-open the nightly schedule and save it once. The re-save is the step that binds the new token — rotating alone leaves the schedule pointed at the expired credential.

If the job still fails after that, the schedule owner's account may be deactivated; reply here and we'll reassign ownership for you.
