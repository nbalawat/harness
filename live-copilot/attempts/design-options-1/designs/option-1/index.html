Customer says their scheduled Atlas export fails every night with token_expired, but manual exports work fine. What do I tell them?

Copilot09:41
Automated — not a human agent3 sources

Scheduled exports authenticate with a service token, which is separate from the interactive session token used for manual exports. Service tokens expire after 90 days and do not auto-renew. [1]

Have the customer rotate the token in Settings → Integrations → Atlas → Rotate service token, then re-save the schedule so the new token is bound to it. Re-saving is required — rotation alone leaves the old token on the schedule. [2] [3]

If the failure continues after rotation, the schedule was likely created by a deactivated user; ownership must be reassigned.

Hi Priya,

Thanks for the detail — the difference between your manual and scheduled exports is the credential each one uses. Manual exports run on your interactive session; scheduled exports run on a service token, which expires after 90 days and does not renew automatically. That expiry is what produces token_expired overnight.

To fix it tonight: open Settings → Integrations → Atlas, choose Rotate service token, then re-open the nightly schedule and save it once. The re-save is the step that binds the new token — rotating alone leaves the schedule pointing at the expired credential.

If the job still fails after that, the schedule owner's account may be deactivated; reply here and we'll reassign ownership for you.
