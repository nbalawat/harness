Verrazano Dental Group seeks $640,000 to consolidate two practice locations and fund operatory build-out. The borrower has operated eleven years under continuous ownership, with trailing-twelve revenue of $4,180,000[1] and adjusted EBITDA of $712,000[2].

Coverage is adequate but not generous. Debt service coverage computes to 1.24×[3] against the policy floor of 1.20×, leaving modest headroom. Leverage of 3.1×[4] sits inside the 4.0× guidance for the practice-management segment. The current ratio of 1.42×[5] reflects seasonal receivable timing rather than structural strain.

Collateral is the two practice properties plus a blanket lien on equipment. The appraised combined value of $780,000 yields a loan-to-value of 82%[6], which exceeds the 75% policy cap[7] and has been raised as a written exception. Absent a waiver, this deal may not advance to signature.

Recommendation: approve subject to resolution of the LTV exception, an annual DSCR covenant at 1.15×, and a personal guarantee from both principals.

Acceptance records your name, the time, and any edits you make. The workflow does not advance until you act.
