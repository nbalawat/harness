The annual plan refund window

A conversation between R. Okafor and the assistant, grounded in six indexed product documents. Follow-ups resolve against everything said above.

R. Okafor
09:12

Customer on an annual plan wants a refund 45 days in. What is our policy?

Assistant
09:12

Annual plans carry a full-refund window of thirty days from purchase. Past that date the plan is non-refundable, though a pro-rated account credit may be issued for the unused remainder at the analyst's discretion.

At forty-five days, this customer sits outside the window; credit is the remedy available.

Does that change if they downgrade instead?

Assistant

A mid-term downgrade converts the unused balance into credit against the next invoice rather than a refund. The documented policy describes no cash-out path.
