Customer on an annual plan wants a refund 45 days in. What's our policy?

AI

Annual plans have a 30-day full-refund window. After day 30 the plan is non-refundable, but you can issue a pro-rated account credit for the unused remainder.

At day 45, this customer is outside the window — credit is the available remedy.
