# Northwind Software — Employee Handbook

## Paid time off (PTO)
Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid vacation per year, accruing at 1.67 days per month. Unused vacation rolls over up to a maximum of 30 days; anything above 30 is forfeited at year end. PTO requests of 3 days or more must be submitted at least two weeks in advance through the People portal. Sick leave is separate and uncapped, but absences of more than 3 consecutive days require a doctor's note.

## Remote work and hours
Northwind is remote-first. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in the employee's local time zone; outside those hours schedules are flexible. Employees may work from anywhere within their country of employment. Working from another country for more than 30 days per year requires prior approval from People Ops for tax and compliance reasons.

## Expenses and reimbursement
Business expenses are reimbursed within 15 business days of an approved submission. Receipts are required for any expense over $25. The home-office stipend is $500 per year for equipment and $50 per month toward internet. Software subscriptions must be approved by a manager before purchase. Travel is booked through the company travel portal; economy fare is standard, business class is permitted only for flights over 6 hours.

## Health and benefits
Medical, dental, and vision coverage begins on the first day of employment — there is no waiting period. Northwind covers 100% of the employee premium and 75% of dependents. The 401(k) match is 100% of the first 4% of salary contributed, vesting immediately. Open enrollment runs the first two weeks of November each year.

## Parental leave
Birthing parents receive 16 weeks of fully paid leave; non-birthing parents receive 12 weeks. Leave must begin within 12 months of the birth or adoption and can be taken in up to three separate blocks. Health benefits continue unchanged during leave.

## Security and devices
All work must be done on a company-issued device with disk encryption and the managed MDM profile enabled. Passwords must be stored in the company password manager; sharing credentials over chat or email is prohibited. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to Security within 4 hours. Phishing reports go to security@northwind.example and are triaged within one business hour.

## Performance and promotion
Performance reviews happen twice a year, in March and September. Promotions are decided in a calibration committee and take effect the month after approval. Compensation is reviewed annually in March; market adjustments may occur off-cycle for retention.
