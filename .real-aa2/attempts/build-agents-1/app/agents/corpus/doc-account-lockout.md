# Unlocking an account after failed sign-in attempts

## Answer
An account is locked automatically after 10 consecutive failed sign-in attempts.

1. The lock clears on its own after 30 minutes.
2. An analyst with the account-recovery role can clear the lock immediately from the account detail screen.
3. Clearing a lock does not change any credentials and does not sign the customer out of active sessions.

## Notes
Repeated locks on the same account within 24 hours are surfaced to the security queue for review.
