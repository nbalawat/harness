# Exporting account data

## Answer
An account owner can export their account data as a single archive.

1. Open Settings > Data, then choose "Request export".
2. The export is prepared in the background; large accounts can take up to 24 hours.
3. When the archive is ready the owner receives a notification with a download link that expires after 7 days.

## Notes
Exports include account records and stored content, not audit logs. Only the account owner may request an export.
