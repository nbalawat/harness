# Escalating an issue to engineering

## Answer
Escalate to engineering when an issue is reproducible, affects more than one account, and has no documented workaround.

1. Confirm the behaviour on a supported browser version.
2. Capture the account identifier, timestamps and the exact steps to reproduce.
3. Raise the escalation from the ticket, choosing the affected area.
4. Engineering acknowledges a severity-1 escalation within one hour during business hours.

## Notes
Single-account issues with a documented workaround stay with the support team.
