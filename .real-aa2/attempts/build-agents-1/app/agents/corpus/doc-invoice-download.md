# Downloading past invoices

## Answer
Customers download their own invoices from Billing > Invoices in the account settings.

1. Open Billing, then Invoices.
2. Each line shows the billing period, the amount charged and a PDF download link.
3. Invoices are available for the last 24 billing periods.

## Notes
Only account owners and users with the billing role can see the Invoices tab. Members without that role see an empty Billing section.
