# Resetting a customer password

## Answer
A customer resets their own password from the sign-in screen; an analyst never needs to set a password on their behalf.

1. On the sign-in screen the customer selects "Forgot password".
2. They enter the email address on the account and submit the form.
3. A reset link is emailed to that address. The link is valid for 60 minutes and can be used once.
4. Following the link, the customer chooses a new password of at least 12 characters.
5. All other active sessions are signed out automatically once the new password is saved.

If the reset email does not arrive within 10 minutes, have the customer check their spam folder and confirm the address on the account is correct, then request the link again.

## Notes
Analysts cannot view or set customer passwords. If the customer cannot receive email at the address on file, the address change has to be verified through the account-recovery path before a reset link will help.
